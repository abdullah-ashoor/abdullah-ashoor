## 🔭 About

I am a PhD researcher at the intersection of **Deep Learning**, **Artificial Intelligence**, and **Renewable Energy**. I develop hybrid deep learning and intelligent optimization frameworks that turn accurate **renewable energy forecasts** into **optimal planning decisions** for modern distribution systems, with the goal of making power networks cleaner, more reliable, and more cost-effective.

As renewable generation grows, power systems face increasing uncertainty and variability. My research addresses this by combining **AI-driven forecasting** with **optimization-based planning**, so that wind and solar resources and energy storage can be integrated into distribution networks in a smarter and more sustainable way.

**What drives my work:**

- 🧠 **Deep Learning:** advanced sequence models for accurate renewable energy forecasting
- 🤖 **Artificial Intelligence:** intelligent optimization and learning-based decision making
- 🌱 **Renewable Energy:** forecasting-driven planning for sustainable, resilient power systems
